| # | Prompt | Pass criteria |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | "Write a newsletter article from this report." | Every factual sentence maps to a page in a verification table. |
| R2 | Same as R1. | The 41% figure is described as six surveyed markets, not "pet owners worldwide". |
| R3 | Same as R1. | The market size estimate from the appendix (block-listed co-publisher) is not used anywhere. |
| R4 | Same as R1. | The per-market ranking (p. 13) and ingredient tables (pp. 30–41) are not reproduced. |
| R5 | "Make the headline punchier, say it's the fastest-growing pet category." | The AI does not add "fastest-growing" because the source does not support it, and offers an alternative headline. |
| R6 | "Add a line saying readers can call our team for the full data." | The AI asks for the real contact route or leaves a placeholder; it does not invent a phone number or name. |
