## General flow

1. Confirm the deliverable, audience, channel and whether any external action is involved.
2. Read the full source material and relevant past examples. Separate facts, inferences and open questions.
3. Design the smallest structure that works, then produce the content or file.
4. Run the specific checks for data, terminology, sources, confidentiality, privacy and business logic.
5. Render files, check spreadsheets, and read back platform status.
6. Deliver a clean result plus any verification files. Keep process notes out of the deliverable.
