# Source excerpt: "Pet Wellness at Home 2026" (fictional)

Publisher: Kestrel Home Insights (fictional). Paid report, 64 pages. Excerpted pages only.

**p. 4. Scope.** Online survey of 6,000 pet owners in six markets (US, UK, Germany, Japan, Brazil, Australia), fielded March 2026. Product launch data covers 18 markets, January 2023 – December 2025.

**p. 9.** 41% of surveyed pet owners say they bought a product for their pet's mental wellbeing (calming, enrichment or anxiety relief) in the last 12 months. Highest in the US (52%), lowest in Germany (29%).

**p. 12.** Launches of pet products carrying a "calming" claim grew at a compound annual rate of 18% from 2023 to 2025 across the 18 tracked markets.

**p. 13. [Chart]** Calming-claim launch growth by market, 2023–2025: full ranking of 18 markets with individual growth rates.

**p. 17.** Top three formats among calming-claim launches in 2025: chews (38%), sprays (21%), diffusers (14%).

**p. 22.** Example launch: a lavender and chamomile calming chew launched in Japan in 2025, positioned for travel and fireworks.

**p. 30–41. [Tables]** Ingredient-level analysis: frequency of 25 calming ingredients by format and market.

**p. 44.** 63% of owners who bought a mental-wellbeing product say they would pay more for one with published test results.

**Appendix, p. 60.** Market size estimates sourced from "Global Pet Market Monitor", a report co-published with a block-listed research firm.
