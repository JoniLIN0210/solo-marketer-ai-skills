# Report preview | Calm pets, busy shelves: what's driving the pet wellness boom

*Fictional example built from [`sample-report-excerpt.md`](sample-report-excerpt.md).*

Pet care used to stop at food, toys and vet visits. Owners now also buy for their pets' mental wellbeing, and product developers are following.

Kestrel Home Insights' report *Pet Wellness at Home 2026* combines a survey of 6,000 pet owners in six markets with product launch data from 18 markets. Below is an excerpt.

## Owners are already buying

In March 2026, 41% of surveyed pet owners said they had bought a product for their pet's mental wellbeing, such as calming, enrichment or anxiety relief, in the previous 12 months. The share varied widely across the six markets surveyed, from 29% in Germany to 52% in the US.

## Launches are following demand

Across the 18 markets tracked, launches of pet products with a "calming" claim grew at a compound annual rate of 18% between 2023 and 2025. Chews were the most common format in 2025, ahead of sprays and diffusers.

One example: a lavender and chamomile calming chew launched in Japan in 2025, positioned for stressful moments such as travel and fireworks.

## Proof matters

Among owners who had bought a mental-wellbeing product, 63% said they would pay more for one with published test results. For product developers, evidence may become as important as the ingredient list.
