# Measuring the impact of AI workflows

"It feels faster" does not survive a budget review. Measure four layers, starting in the first week.

| Layer | Question | Example metrics |
|---|---|---|
| Adoption | Is the workflow actually used? | Share of eligible tasks run through the skill; number of people using it |
| Efficiency | Does it save time? | Time per task; time from request to delivery |
| Quality | Is the output reliable? | Factual errors found after delivery; leaks in contact lists; rounds of revision |
| Business | Does it move results? | Leads, conversion, search and AI-answer visibility, pipeline influenced |

## Practical rules

- **Record a baseline first.** Time three runs of the task the old way before switching.
- **Count failures honestly.** Log every bad output, what caused it (input data, instructions, model capability or task design) and how it was fixed.
- **Attribute carefully.** Business results usually come from several efforts at once. Say what share AI plausibly contributed rather than claiming the whole result.
- **Compare like with like.** When testing a change, keep the audience, timing and channel the same, or use a holdout group.
